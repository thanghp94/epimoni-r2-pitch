APPENDIX · Q&A BACKUP — 1/3

Hostile questions, owned answers.

"Why won't students just use ChatGPT?"DN
ChatGPT optimizes for finishing; we optimize for learning. Schools want a guardrailed alternative.
"You're 13. Why you?"ALL
We ARE the user — we live the 9pm problem nightly. Competitors guess; we know. Plus a supervising teacher.
"What if schools won't pay?"TC
The pilot is free — we sell proof, not promises. Fallback: ~199k VND/quarter parent-direct.
"How is this different from Khan / Quizlet / Duolingo?"GM
Khan = content, Quizlet = drill, Duolingo = streaks. None coaches how to learn — a layer they don't occupy.
"Where's your data?"LH
Prototype works today; pilot data starts this term. Design rests on published evidence — we never invent numbers.
"Screen time? Parents?"GM
A 3–5 minute closed loop designed to end — the opposite of engagement-max apps.
"AI safety for kids?"DN
Teacher-validated misconception matrix, scripted hint ladders, no open chat, no personal data.
"What stops a big edtech copying you?"LH
The moat is misconception matrices + pilot data — craft and evidence, not code. We're faster: zero burn.
"Is 5 minutes enough to learn anything?"GM
The daily rep, not the whole workout — distributed reps beat marathon sessions. Quests chain daily.
"Why would a student open it without being forced?"TC
One button, five minutes, an immediate win — and schools can assign quests instead of homework.
"You want to replace IXL? You're four students."LH
We start beneath them as the coach layer. Schools keep what they like; we consolidate spend as we prove gains.
"Isn't XP just Duolingo?"GM
We reward the process — retrieval, correction, calibration — not completion. Points sit on intrinsic design; never pay-to-skip.
"Isn't this just AI-generated lessons?"DN
AI drafts; the validated misconception matrix is the quality gate. The product isn't a lesson — it's consistency.
"OpenAI ships this feature tomorrow — over?"GM
Then the category is validated. OpenAI sells tokens; our moat is the validated matrix + school channel + proof-of-learning data.
"Kids farm XP by failing on purpose?"GM
Wrong-then-right still means the workout happened — struggle IS the learning. Calibration checks and transfer quests can't be farmed.
